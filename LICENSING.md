# CozyClay licensing

CozyClay is distributed under the MIT license. The complete license text is in
[`LICENSE`](LICENSE).

The Studio footer keeps a source-code link. Operators deploying a modified
version can point it at their own repository by setting `VITE_SOURCE_CODE_URL`
at build time. (MIT does not require this offer — it is kept as a courtesy.)

## History

Before November 2026, CozyClay was published under `AGPL-3.0-or-later` (and
before 2026-08-21, `GPL-3.0-or-later`). The copyright holders have agreed to
relicense the combined work, including earlier contributions, under MIT.
Releases published before the change remain available under the license they
were distributed under.
